# Student Guide

## Exercise 1: Understand your agents

Open the Agents screen and answer:

- Who is your Chief of Staff?
- Which agents are technical?
- Which agents are creative?
- Which agents should be paused?

## Exercise 2: Understand the task board

Open the Task Board and find the four categories:

- Projetos Planejados
- Doing
- Review
- Done

Then inspect `~/.openclaw/workspace/tasks/index.json` and `~/.openclaw/workspace/PROJETOS.md` to understand where the cards came from.

## Exercise 3: Audit costs

Open Cost & Model Tracker and answer:

- Which model costs the most?
- Which agent is using the most tokens?
- Is pricing coverage at 100%?
- Where is `model-prices.json` stored?

## Exercise 4: Design your own module

Pick one:

- content pipeline;
- sales pipeline;
- research tracker;
- customer support inbox;
- study planner;
- local business operations board.
